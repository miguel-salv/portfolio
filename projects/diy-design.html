DIY Design and Fabrication

24-672 at CMU

Mechanical

CMU

This class at CMU offered me a deep dive into DIY product design and fabrication processes. I learned the design process through engineering sketches, and I learned various fabrication methods such as laser cutting.


Project 1: Cup Holder

The goal of this project was to design a cup holder designed to be sleek, portable, and lightweight. I designed the cupholder to be made out of a single sheet of acrylic which was then shaped into the holder using a heat gun. The handle was designed in a way which made it possible to carry on a backpack.


Project 2: Transforming Box

The goal of this project was to design a paper container that transforms into another object with a different purpose. Inspired by my interest in rock climbing, I designed a box that transports your climbing chalk to the gym and then transforms into a phone holder to record your climbs. The box was made by laser cutting a piece of matboard and includes removable slots to hold your phone.
